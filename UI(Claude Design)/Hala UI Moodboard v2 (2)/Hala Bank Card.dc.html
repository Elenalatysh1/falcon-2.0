Bank card

How Hala cards appear in the app, in wallets and in network reviews. Based on Visa's digital card standards, the Mastercard Brand Center, mada's brand guidelines and PCI masking rules. The card art carries the real Visa and mada marks. Standalone network logos in sections 07 and 08 are labelled placeholders until the official files are added.

06

Sizes

The mada logo must be at least 60 px wide, so that sets the smallest card size that can show it. Smaller sizes scale the same card art; below 96 px, show the card name and last four digits beside it instead.

Card number field

For paying with an external card (top-up, eCommerce). Groups of four, left-to-right in every language. The network is detected from the first six digits; mada takes priority on co-badged cards and its logo appears inside the field.
