Money components

The eight components behind "Money first" and "Say what happens": how amounts look, how they're typed, how a payment is reviewed, approved, confirmed and tracked. Teams build on these instead of making their own. Every example with a "Live" tag works, and many are connected: the amount you type flows into the review and the approval rule.
